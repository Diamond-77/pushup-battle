Pushup Battle Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

What we collect

When you create an account we store your email address, the player name you choose, and the profile picture you upload, if any. If you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and Google account ID from Google.

Your camera

The camera is used only to count your push-ups. The video is processed on your own device and is never recorded, uploaded or stored.

Matches

During a match your player name, picture and rep count are shared live with your opponent. Other online players can see your player name, picture and whether you are online or in a match.

Where data is stored

Account data is stored with our database provider, Supabase. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and data, email the address on the app's Google sign-in screen and we will remove it.
